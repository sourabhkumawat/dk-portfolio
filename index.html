About Me
Built for
architecture.
Made for cinema.

I'm Dharendra Ranka — a Mumbai-based architect, interior designer, and art director who has spent 13 years building spaces that people live in, and 6+ years building worlds that people watch.

Architecture taught me to think in three dimensions before a single brick is laid. That same instinct is what I bring to every film set — reading a script and feeling the space it needs, translating that vision into precise drawings, then standing on the floor making sure every corner, every prop, every beam of light tells the story it's supposed to tell.

I believe great sets are never just backgrounds. They breathe. They hold the character's truth. That's what I build.
